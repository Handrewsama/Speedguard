# SpeedGuard Halo V2

- Siri-inspired perimeter-only light.
- Thin luminous frame concentrated at the screen edge.
- Moving light beam travels around the perimeter.
- Green calm state is slow/subtle.
- Warning/multa states are warmer and faster.
- Serious/penal states increase brightness, glow and speed.
- Keeps `pointer-events:none`; it does not block controls.
- Includes fallback for browsers without `color-mix`.

This is still a web/PWA visual prototype. iOS cannot draw this over another app such as Maps/Waze; native background operation will be addressed in the iOS phase.
